# Demand Radar outbound system

## Positioning rule

Do not sound like an SEO agency, ads agency, lead-gen shop, or generic growth consultant.

The core message:

Seyko shows what patients are asking, how nearby surgeons earn trust, and where qualified consults may be hesitating.

## Target buyer

- Founder-led plastic surgery practices
- Premium practices with visible branding investment
- High-ticket procedure mix: rhinoplasty, facelift, mommy makeover, revision, body contouring
- Likely already spending on SEO, ads, content, or agency support
- Operators who may suspect demand is there but do not know why it is not turning into enough qualified consults

## Research inputs before sending

- Procedure focus visible on the site
- Local competitors ranking or positioning around the same procedure
- Patient concerns from Reddit, Google autocomplete, reviews, forums, and People Also Ask
- Evidence of consult hesitation: pricing ambiguity, revision anxiety, unclear surgeon-selection proof, thin before/after context, generic trust language
- One specific reason the practice may be missing consult demand

## Strong observation pattern

Use this structure:

1. We noticed a specific patient behavior.
2. Most practices are answering it weakly.
3. That creates a reason patients may hesitate.
4. We can send a short Demand Radar breakdown.

## Observation bank

Use these as starting points after real research. Replace the market, procedure, and evidence with what you actually found.

## Call opener

## Diagnostic close

The one-time Demand Radar Diagnostic is a 7-day read on:

- patient demand read
- competitor read
- consult hesitation review
- more consult opportunities
- procedure page priorities
- owner briefing

The output is not "more marketing ideas." It is a clear view of what patients care about, where the practice is harder to choose, and what deserves attention first.

## Retainer close

Ongoing Demand Radar is for practice owners who want a sharper monthly read. Each month, we monitor patient concerns, competitor movement, procedure demand, and consult economics so the practice can make growth decisions with more clarity.
